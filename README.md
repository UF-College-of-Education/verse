# Welcome to VERSE

VERSE is a collaborative project with the local community, co-designing virtual reality experiences for practicing real-world skills and promoting inclusivity and understanding.

## Emerging Technologies Research Lab

The Emerging Technologies Research Lab (ELTL) focuses on collaborative opportunities and critical discourse to enable greater connections with our community to co-design research for equitable and advanced technologies. Our aim is to bolster inclusion and provide solutions to real-world problems with cutting-edge learning technologies.

## About VERSE

Welcome to VERSE (Virtual Environments for Realistic Simulated Experiences). This project is based on the idea that virtual environments can be a safe space to rehearse and practice skills, especially for people who might find real-world activities anxiety-inducing or worrying. Not all places are attuned to people with neurodiversity.

With this in mind, we have collaborated with our local autistic community to co-design apps that help enable and support access to a range of environments. By working with local communities, we also aim to educate and help make the real world more inclusive and understanding.

This first virtual reality experience, visiting a restaurant (Piesanos), is interactive and choice-based. You decide how to proceed through the experience. Most importantly, it was co-created and co-designed with the autistic community. We see no value in designing these experiences without vital community input that includes autistic voices in defining challenges, in addition to employing and featuring autistic input throughout the design and development process.

We are very grateful to Jacob Warshofsky for his input and expert insights that helped shape this VR experience. Further research will follow, but for now, enjoy this free VR experience.

## Supported Platforms

You can use VERSE in a web browser, or on an Android phone coupled with a Google Cardboard VR headset. If you require one of these headsets, please contact us and we will mail one out for free.

### Web / PC / Mac

The experience is viewable on desktop web browsers.

To view it:

1. Open the experience in your browser.
2. Click the VR button to enable full screen mode.
3. Click and drag to navigate the scene.
4. Hover the gaze cursor over choices to make selections.

Experience URL:

https://education.ufl.edu/verse/experience/restaurant/

### iOS

Cardboard viewer mode is currently disabled for iOS. The experience is still viewable using gyroscope-enabled AR mode with the Mozilla WebXR Viewer app.

To use it:

1. Download the Mozilla WebXR Viewer app from the App Store.
2. Once downloaded, open the browser and navigate to:
	https://education.ufl.edu/verse/experience/restaurant/
3. Tap the XR button and enable gyroscopic controls when prompted.
4. Once enabled, you will be able to use your phone's gyroscope to control the view into the Piesanos scene.
5. To make selections, place the gaze cursor on an option and hold it until the selection is made.

### Android

On Android, the experience is usable in Cardboard viewer mode.

To run the experience:

1. Due to autoplay limitations, the experience will only run on Firefox and Samsung Browser.
2. If you do not have either installed, download one from the Google Play Store.
3. Once downloaded, navigate to:
	https://education.ufl.edu/verse/experience/restaurant/
4. Tap the VR button.
5. When prompted, turn your phone sideways and place it in your Cardboard or third-party headset.
6. Once in the headset, select choices in the experience by looking at them with your gaze cursor.
